# Veil interface direction

The existing Veil frontend is the visual source of truth for this port.

- Identity: discreet security research desk, not a generic crypto dashboard.
- Personality: technical, restrained, private, and precise.
- Palette: near-black surfaces, warm white type, muted grey support text, and teal reserved for proof state and primary actions.
- Typography: editorial serif display type for narrative emphasis, neutral sans for reading, and mono only for addresses, proofs, and chain data.
- Mood: focused and forensic rather than promotional.
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

Major decisions retain their purpose from the existing interface: the dark canvas reduces glare during code-heavy work; teal marks verified or actionable proof state; sparse square geometry supports the security-tool character; motion is limited to state transitions and the proof pipeline.
